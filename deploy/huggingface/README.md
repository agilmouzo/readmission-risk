Interactive demo of the [readmission-risk](https://github.com/agilmouzo/readmission-risk)
project: a LightGBM model that estimates the chance that a diabetic patient is readmitted
within 30 days of discharge.

Educational project, **not a clinical tool**. Trained on US hospital data from 1999–2008.
